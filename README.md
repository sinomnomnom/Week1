## Reflection ##
In this game I was inspired by both the gameplay and theming of the classic pinball machines I played as research, and I started with the gameplay. Knew I wanted to add rails, because I really liked how satisfying it is when I would hit the ball hard at a ramp and it would curve back around the ramp smoothly keeping the same speed, and coming back with even more speed. I spent a lot of time trying to make my rails good, and they are mostly good but not perfect quite yet. I wanted something like in Black Knight 2000, where you can keep the ball going in a loop around a rail with an upper flipper, gaining more and more speed (and points) until you miss. I also liked having a goal to progress to, and I knew I wanted multiball, so I decided to add an evil wizard tower where I could trap balls inside and then release them all once I had 3 trapped. Multiball is a really good mechanic in pinball, and I could combine it with the progression of destroying the tower, something else I liked from Medieval Madness. I also liked the trolls from medieval madness, so I put evil tree monsters that pop up from the ground to defend the evil wizard, and you have to enchant your ball with the good wizards power (a magnoring) of power and happiness to damage them.
For the art I wanted to use a similar style to Tolkein's illustrations in The Hobbit, but it took a really long time because I lost my drawing tablet pen so I used my mouse. If i were to redo it I would probably just use Tolkein's illustrations directly, because it would be faster and look nicer.
